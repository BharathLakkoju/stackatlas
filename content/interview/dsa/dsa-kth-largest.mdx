Find the kth largest element in an unsorted array.

## Model answer

Min-heap size k: for each num, push; if size>k pop smallest. Final peek is kth largest. O(n log k) time, O(k) space—good for small k or streams.

Quickselect partitions like quicksort around pivot targeting index (n-k). Average O(n), worst O(n²) without random pivot.

Full sort O(n log n) is baseline. Clarify kth largest with duplicates counts multiset.

**Complexity:** Heap O(n log k); quickselect O(n) average.

## Follow-ups

### k near n?

Large k reduces heap advantage; quickselect still reasonable.

### Duplicates?

Unless problem says distinct, duplicates count separately.
