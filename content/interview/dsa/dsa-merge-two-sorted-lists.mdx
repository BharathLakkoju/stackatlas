Merge two ascending linked lists and return the merged list head.

## Model answer

Use dummy node and tail pointer. While both heads exist, attach smaller node to tail.next, advance that list and tail. Attach remaining non-null list.

Relink existing nodes—no new nodes unless required. O(n+m) time, O(1) auxiliary space iterative.

Recursive merge is O(n+m) stack depth—prefer iterative in interviews unless asked.

**Complexity:** Time O(n+m), space O(1).

## Follow-ups

### Recursive merge?

Recursive: O(n+m) call stack; iterative preferred for deep lists.

### Merge k lists?

Min-heap of k heads: pop smallest, push next; O(N log k) total nodes N.
