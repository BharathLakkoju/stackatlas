Design home feed of posts from followees.

## Model answer

Posts table; follows graph. Fan-out on write pushes post ids into follower feed caches—fast reads, costly for mega-followers.

Fan-out on read merges timelines at request—cheap writes, heavy reads.

Hybrid: fan-out normal users; merge celebrities at read. Rank blend recency + engagement; cursor pagination.


## Follow-ups

### Celebrity users?

No fan-out for celebrity posts; merge on read.

### Ranking freshness?

Score = weighted recency + engagement with decay.
