Email, push, SMS for product events.

## Model answer

Event → orchestrator loads preferences, renders template, enqueues per channel.

Workers call SES/FCM/Twilio with retries and DLQ.

Idempotency keys skip duplicate sends under at-least-once delivery.


## Follow-ups

### Deduplication?

Store processed idempotency hash 24-72h.

### Priority lanes?

Separate critical vs marketing queues.
