When choose ECS over EKS on AWS?

## Model answer

ECS is AWS-native orchestration—simpler ops, tight IAM and ALB integration, good for teams without K8s expertise. EKS provides portable Kubernetes—multi-cloud, rich ecosystem (operators, helm), higher operational burden (control plane, node upgrades, CNI). ECS on Fargate removes host management similar to EKS Fargate. Pick ECS for straightforward microservices on AWS; EKS when you need K8s APIs, GitOps already on K8s, or multi-cloud. Cost: EKS control plane hourly fee plus nodes; ECS no cluster fee. See [ECS vs EKS](/compare/ecs-vs-eks).


## Follow-ups

### Fargate?

Fargate serverless tasks/pods—no EC2 management; cold task start still applies.

### Service mesh?

App mesh/Istio on EKS adds mTLS observability complexity.
