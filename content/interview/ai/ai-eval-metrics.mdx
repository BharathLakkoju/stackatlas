What metrics evaluate a RAG system?

## Model answer

Retrieval: recall@k, MRR on labeled question-chunk pairs. Generation: faithfulness/groundedness (answer supported by context), citation accuracy, hallucination rate. End-to-end task success with human or rubric LLM judge. Track latency and cost per query. Regression suite on every index or prompt change. Online: thumbs, implicit signals, escalation rate. Balance automatic metrics with periodic human audit—LLM judges biased toward fluent wrong answers.


## Follow-ups

### LLM-as-judge?

LLM judge cheap at scale but calibrate against human gold.

### Offline vs online?

Offline CI blocks deploy; online dashboards catch drift.
