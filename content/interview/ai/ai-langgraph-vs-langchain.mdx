When prefer LangGraph over LangChain chains?

## Model answer

LangGraph models agents as explicit graph—nodes, edges, conditional routing, persistent checkpoint state, human interrupts. Good for multi-step workflows needing recovery and audit. Classic LangChain LCEL chains are lighter for linear retrieve-then-generate. Use graph when cycles, branching, or durable state matter; chains for simple pipelines. Both share tool bindings—choice is orchestration complexity. Details: [compare](/compare/langchain-vs-langgraph).
