Consistent hashing is how real caches and CDNs limit **remap churn** when you scale out or replace a node. This lab places keys on a ring and shows which server owns a key before and after topology changes.

## What to notice

- **Adding a node** should move only keys near that segment—not the whole keyspace.
- **Removing a node** reassigns its arc to the next clockwise server—plan for temporary overload on that neighbor.
- **Virtual nodes** spread hot physical machines across multiple points on the ring, reducing skew.

## Interview prompts

After the lab, explain:

1. Why modulo hashing `hash(key) % N` is painful when N changes.
2. How you would detect and mitigate a **hot key** even with consistent hashing.
3. What happens during a network **partition** between two cache halves (PACELC: latency vs consistency).

Optional: estimate blast radius with [/tools/capacity](/tools/capacity) for a shard count change.
