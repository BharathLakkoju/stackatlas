Chunking is the cheapest RAG knob and the easiest to get wrong. This lab splits sample documentation with adjustable **size**, **overlap**, and **structure-aware breaks** (headings vs raw characters). Retrieval scores update against a small golden question set baked into the lab.

## What to notice

- **Tiny chunks** improve pinpoint questions but drop surrounding context the model needs to synthesize.
- **Large chunks** embed dilute topics—cosine similarity peaks for the wrong paragraph.
- **Overlap** reduces boundary cuts through sentences but multiplies index size and ingest cost.
- **Markdown-aware splits** often beat fixed character windows on internal wikis.
